# Home page (arsyalavina.web.id)

Scope: the single-page personal site. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), with a persuade close (contact).
Audience: recruiters, freelance clients, and peers, equally. Job: understand Lavina's focus, see real projects, reach him.
Proof: five CV projects (three with screenshots), UNJAYA internship, Amikom and SMK education, CV skills. Nothing invented.
Constraints: bilingual EN/ID toggle; calm, simple, colourful; previous cream + pastel + serif-italic attempt rejected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a length of batik cap cloth taken through its dye baths. Each section is one bath, and the cloth darkens from deep nila through pale first-dip blue to a final soga brown. It refuses the neutral ground with a hero, a card grid, and an accent colour.

OWN-WORLD: Nila indigo drench (#1f3a6b family), pale nila first-dip (#d6e1ee), unwaxed mori cloth tinted cool (#eef2f6), soga brown (#6b3f22) with isen gold (#e0b25a). Wax-white resist type (#f4f1ea) on dyed grounds. The kawung cap stamp appears only as the pinggiran border band where one bath meets the next, plus one exception: the header logo, where it is the maker's cap mark. Other markers are isen cecek dots. Flat dye fields, no gradients, no glass, no cards.

STORY: Visitors meet Lavina's name written in wax on indigo. They see all five projects indexed straight away, read each one with its role and stack, follow one unbroken path from SMK 2020 to the 2026 projects, then reach the soga contact bath and email or download the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width nila field. The name is set very large in wax-white across the left two-thirds, with title and one-sentence summary below it. Email (gold) and Resume sit directly under the summary. A full-colour formal portrait is on the right (the user chose colour over the original nila duotone). Along the bottom runs a stamped index of the five projects, each one linking to its row.

FORM: Celup Batik Cap, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list, seed a019ceb8. Raises: active emptiness (ikebana), a strict margin column carrying role and year (orizuru), and one unbroken timeline line (drum machine step row). Signature interaction: pinggiran stamps press in one by one as a bath boundary enters the viewport. The EN/ID toggle swaps copy in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
