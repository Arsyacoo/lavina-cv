# Home page (Arsyacoo)

Scope: the bilingual applied-AI studio landing page at `/` and `/id`. Visitor mode: Persuade, with a proof-led experience and a direct contact close.

Audience: potential collaborators, clients, recruiters, and technical peers. Job: understand what Arsyacoo builds, see work that makes the claim credible, and start a conversation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Arsyacoo is an AI workbench / evidence board. The page shows the mechanism around the model — question, source, answer, human review, and next action — then backs it with real project surfaces.

FIRST VIEWPORT: dark ink hero with the brand statement “AI for work that matters.” on the left and a source-grounded PDF Insight AI flow panel on the right. Primary action goes to proof; secondary action opens email. A lime signal mark is the only brand ornament.

WORLD: dark ink (`#0b1220`), raised ink (`#131e30`), cool paper (`#f8fbf9`), fog (`#eef3f4`), lime signal (`#c5f04c`), coral close (`#f16f5d`), teal secondary trace (`#8de4dc`). Bricolage Grotesque display, Hanken Grotesk body, hairline rules, 7px controls, 14px media surfaces.

STORY: visitors meet the product thesis, see one concrete AI document flow, inspect six existing projects, understand the three working principles and stack, meet the founder, then reach a short contact close.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the hero evidence panel reveals with a single upward motion and keeps a restrained cursor blink in the answer state. Reduced-motion users see the settled state.

CONSTRAINTS: preserve EN/ID routes, current project links, screenshots, resume, social links, and factual project content. Do not invent clients, metrics, testimonials, awards, or deployment claims.
